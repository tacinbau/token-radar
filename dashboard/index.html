How this radar works

1. Fitted comps curve, not a fixed multiple. For the — token-bearing protocols with a market cap, log10(market cap) is regressed on log10(TVL), plus a shrunken category offset (each category’s mean residual, shrunk by n/(n+5), so thin categories borrow the global curve): —.

2. Tested held out, not in sample. 5-fold cross-validation: each of the 128 comps is predicted once by a curve fitted without it. Median |log10 error| —, — of held-out values within 2×, 90% interval coverage —.

3. Launch odds from the peers’ record. The odds are the share of protocols this old that already record a token, smoothed toward the overall rate: —. Age unknown uses the overall rate. This is the peers’ record, not a promise a token will launch.

4. Costed farm verdicts. Expected farmer value assumes a 10% airdrop pool split among 2,000 farmers. Farm cost is 3 gas sessions (by chain) plus $25 test capital. Verdict: farm when farmer value is ≥20× cost, thin at 5–20×, skip below 5×. Wrapped, staked, bridged and versioned products are always skip, with the reason on the row.

Figures are this entry’s valuation model applied to a frozen snapshot, not a launch forecast and not financial advice. Verify eligibility on the protocol’s own pages before farming.
