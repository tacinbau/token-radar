How the value is scored

TVL times the category comp multiple, adjusted by 7-day growth and by days tracked with no token. This is a hypothetical token market capitalization, not an airdrop allocation.

01 / Category compsMedian market cap ÷ TVL among token-bearing protocols with both figures positive, in the same category. Categories without comps use the global median. Category multiples are winsorized at the 90th percentile of all token-bearing market cap ÷ TVL comparables in the snapshot. Each detail shows its sample size.

02 / Growth1 + clamped 7-day TVL change ÷ 200. Changes are capped at −50% and +100%, giving factors from 0.75× to 1.50×. Missing growth is neutral.

03 / Tracking age1 + min(days tracked, 1,460) ÷ 1,460 × 0.25. Missing dates are neutral. Tracking age is a proxy, not a launch date or proof of token status.

No implied value exceeds the largest token market cap actually recorded in the snapshot. Round to whole dollars; ties favor larger TVL then slug, with a $1 downward adjustment if necessary.
