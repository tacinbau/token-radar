How this radar works

1. Fitted size curve, not a fixed multiple. For the — token-bearing protocols with a market cap, log10(market cap) is regressed on log10(TVL): —. The category effect, 7-day growth, age and chain count were each tested against this curve and rejected — see the evidence tables above.

2. Launch odds from the peers’ record. The odds are the share of protocols this old that already record a token, smoothed toward the overall rate: —. Age unknown uses the overall rate. This is the peers’ record, not a promise a token will launch.

3. Costed farm verdicts, re-priced by your scenario. Farmer gross is the implied value × the drop share (default 7%) split among 2,000 farmers. Net of fees subtracts 3 gas sessions (by chain) and the opportunity cost of the deposit plan (4.5% a year over the horizon). Verdict: farm when net is ≥$25 an hour, marginal when net is ≥$0, skip below $0. Move the drop-share or horizon slider and every row re-prices in place; the board verdict is the default scenario (7% drop, $1,000 deposit, 12 weeks).

Figures are this entry’s valuation model applied to a frozen snapshot, not a launch forecast and not financial advice. “Records no token” means the snapshot records none — a protocol may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry.
